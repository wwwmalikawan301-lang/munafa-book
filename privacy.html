Munafa Book Privacy Policy

Developer contact: www.malikawan301@gmail.com

Munafa Book stores shop records such as product names, prices, stock, sales, customer names and phone numbers, payments, wallets, suppliers, expenses, and shop settings in Firebase Firestore configured for the app. Product thumbnails and full images may also be stored.

Use of data

Data is used to provide stock, sales, udhaar khata, wallet/drawer tracking, staff access, and reports per shop.

Retention and deletion

Records remain stored until the shop owner deletes them or uses in-app shop data deletion, which deletes shop data and attempts account removal. You can also request deletion by emailing the developer contact above.

Security

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Local PIN is a screen lock only. Technical users with Firebase code knowledge may inspect accessible fields; full field-level hiding requires backend functions.

Contact

For questions or to request deletion of your data, email www.malikawan301@gmail.com.
